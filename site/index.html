How we built it

Three parts on a shelf

No custom circuit boards. Milo is built from parts that plug together.

Single-board computer

Pi 4

Milo's brain. Runs the software that listens, thinks and replies.

USB microphone

plug and play

Listens for the wake word "Milo" and picks up what you say next.

Bookshelf speakers

wired pair

Plays Milo's replies loud and clear from the shelf.
